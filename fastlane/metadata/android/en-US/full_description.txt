MealBox is a minimalist, low-stimulus meal and hydration tracker specifically designed to reduce the cognitive load of tracking your nutrition.

Whether in daily life or during stressful periods, MealBox completely avoids calorie counting, complicated macros, or cluttered interfaces. Instead, the app focuses on the essentials: when did you eat what, and how much did you drink?

**Features:**
- 🛡️ **Safe Foods:** A dedicated vault for familiar foods that always work, reducing decision fatigue.
- 💧 **Hydration Tracker:** A simple counter for your daily water intake.
- ⚡ **Energy Levels:** Optional tracking of your energy (High, Mid, Low) during meals.
- 📴 **100% Offline & Local:** Your data belongs to you. There is no cloud, no servers, no registration, and no ads.
- 📱 **Android Widget:** Quickly log meals directly from your home screen.
- 💾 **Export & Import:** A complete backup system allows you to securely save your data locally as a JSON file.

MealBox is entirely Open Source and respects your privacy.
